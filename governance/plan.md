Coverage requires explicitly distinguishing a matched warning from a policy deny,
conditional blocking among detections from blocking among all attack-labeled cases,
logical quarantine from process isolation, descriptor mutation from hidden remote
behavior, and designed-case labels from demonstrated language-model exploitation.
No training or rule tuning uses the scored examples. The existing rules are frozen;
all authored cases are specification-aware, and the source cases are already known.
