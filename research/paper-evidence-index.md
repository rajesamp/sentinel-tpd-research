# Paper evidence index: Sentinel-TPD

Index date: 2026-10-07. This is a human-readable traceability index for the published manuscript and research artifact, not a new experiment, a change to the evaluated code, or an independent human peer review. It covers every numbered table (1–10), figure (1–5), main/appendix section, displayed equation, quantitative claim family and bibliography key in the pinned paper. Scientific statements are mapped to their existing evidence; unavailable or merely proposed material is marked explicitly.
